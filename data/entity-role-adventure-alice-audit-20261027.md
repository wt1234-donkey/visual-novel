# A 段专题职责集中核查（2026-10-27）

依冻结索引顺序一次审查 **111 条**：Adventure International 36 条、Alicesoft 75 条。
复用 8 份当事方原件。Alicesoft 2020 年《HENTAI LABYRINTH》的品牌企划制作协力有具名来源，
另有 6 部作品仅确认 Alice Blue／AliceNoir 子品牌制作归属；父专题的对应职责仍待核。
其余 110 条维持待核，全库待核专题职责 590 → 589。审计中的查阅不等于逐作开发已证。

## 原件包与限度

- [E-A-AI-SAGA](https://www.mocagh.org/ai/saga-manual.pdf)：original 1981 Graphic Adventure manual, p.2 credits。Graphic Adventure graphic engines name Scott Adams, Penguin Software, Mark Pelczarski and Adventure International separately; this later edition is not the 1978 original.
- [E-A-AI-DEATH](https://www.mocagh.org/ai/deathplanet-manual.pdf)：Death Planet original manual, p.1。Adventure International is listed as publisher; Lance Micklus is author and Charles Paul de Young is credited for the Color Computer conversion.
- [E-A-ALICE-HISTORY](https://www.alicesoft.com/outline/contents.html)：company outline, 1983 establishment and 1989 brand start。Alicesoft brand started in 1989, so Champion Soft products in 1982–88 cannot be relabelled as contemporary Alicesoft-branded development.
- [E-A-ALICE-FREE](https://www.alicesoft.com/about/)：free-distribution list, three named Showa Champion Soft products。Little Princess, Little Vampire and Gakuen Senki are explicitly Champion Soft brand products; the text does not assign the later Alicesoft brand development.
- [E-A-ALICE-SUPPORT](https://www.alicesoft.com/support/sys39v549.html)：official System 3.8/3.9 compatibility list, five Alice Blue titles and Nor。The page names Alice Blue and Alice Noir on individual titles; it is a product/engine list, not evidence that the parent brand independently made all components.
- [E-A-ALICE-NOR](https://noir.alicesoft.com/nor/index.htm)：Nor contemporary product page and development diary。The product identifies AliceNoir as its first release; this does not resolve a parent Alicesoft production credit.
- [E-A-ALICE-HENTAI](https://www.alicesoft.com/information/2020/entry002611.html)：2020-07-22 original director announcement, introduction and new-brand paragraphs。The director calls IMYUIC an Alicesoft-produced new brand for this specific game; credits are limited to production collaboration, not independent engine/programming.
- [E-A-ALICE-INDEPENDENCE](https://www.alicesoft.com/information/2022/entry003001.html)：2022-05-30 separation announcement, name and ongoing sale paragraphs。Later IMYUIC(ALICESOFT) sale name and independence cannot be projected backward as sole original development.
